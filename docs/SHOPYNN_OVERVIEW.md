# Shopynn: run your whole shop from your phone

Shopynn is a shop management app for retailers in Ghana. It brings your sales, stock, MoMo payments, customer credit, branches, staff and online orders into one place, on your Android phone and in any web browser.

## Who it is for

Shops that buy and sell physical goods and want to stop guessing:

- Provision shops, minimarts and supermarkets
- Boutiques, cosmetics, phone and electronics shops
- Building materials, hardware and spare parts dealers
- Wholesalers and distributors
- Any business with more than one branch, or staff selling while the owner is away

## The problems we solve

- **Stock and cash never match.** Notebooks, WhatsApp and Excel sheets get out of date. At the end of the day nobody knows exactly what was sold or what is left.
- **You cannot see your shop when you are not there.** Owners have to call the shop to know how the day is going.
- **MoMo payments are hard to confirm.** "I have paid" disputes and end-of-day MoMo reconciliation waste time and money.
- **Credit customers are forgotten.** Who owes what lives in someone's head or an old book.
- **Staff mistakes and theft go unnoticed.** Shared logins mean nobody knows who gave a discount or refunded a sale.
- **Branches move stock by word of mouth.** One branch shows stock that has already left.
- **Goods run out or expire.** Nobody notices until a customer asks or the goods are spoiled.
- **Customers want to order without calling.** WhatsApp orders get lost and stock is double-sold.

## What Shopynn does best

1. **Built for how Ghanaian shops sell.** Take MoMo at the counter: the customer approves on their own phone and the payment is recorded against the sale automatically. Cash, credit and receipts shared on WhatsApp all work the same way.
2. **Your phone is the till.** No computer or special hardware needed. Scan barcodes with the phone camera, add items by voice, and keep selling for cash when the internet drops. Sales sync when you are back online.
3. **See your shop from anywhere.** Today's sales, stock levels and alerts are live on your phone, for every branch.
4. **Stock you can trust.** Every sale, purchase, return, transfer and adjustment is recorded, so you can see exactly why a number changed. Stock counts fix differences properly.
5. **Control over your staff.** Each person has their own login and only sees what their role allows. You can review sales that need approval, see which devices are signed in, and get an alert when a sale is refunded.
6. **Your own online shop.** Customers order from your shop link or the Shopynn app, pay with MoMo, and you fulfil from the same stock.
7. **Safe by default.** The app locks with fingerprint or face, payout details cannot be changed without a code sent to you, and every business's data is kept separate.

## What we want to achieve

Give every Ghanaian shop owner the same control over their business that large supermarkets have: knowing what they sold, what they have, who owes them and what their staff did, from a phone and at a price a small shop can afford.

## Features

### Selling

- Fast checkout on phone or computer, with cash, MoMo and credit
- MoMo at the counter: customer approves on their phone, and slow payments can be parked while you serve the next customer
- Hold a sale and finish it later
- Barcode scanning with the phone camera, and adding items by voice
- Keep selling for cash with no internet; sales sync when you reconnect
- Receipts shared on WhatsApp, A4 invoices, and thermal receipt printing
- Returns and refunds that put stock back
- Daily sales totals and full sales history
- Sales approval for staff where needed

### Stock

- Products with photos, prices, cost, barcode and stock level
- Categories to keep large catalogs tidy
- Bulk product import
- Low-stock reorder list and expiring-soon list
- Full movement history for every product
- Stock counts and adjustments with reasons
- Product catalog export to CSV

### Buying

- Suppliers and their contact details
- Record purchases so stock and cost update automatically
- Purchase returns
- Purchase orders, then receive goods against them

### Customers and credit

- Customer list with purchase and payment history
- Sell on credit and collect payments later
- Outstanding balances at a glance

### Branches

- Several stores or branches, each with its own stock
- Transfer stock between branches with send and receive confirmation
- Switch between branches in the app

### Staff and security

- A login for every staff member, and you can disable leavers
- Roles and permissions (for example, a cashier who cannot give refunds)
- Fingerprint or face lock on the app
- List of signed-in devices, with remote sign-out
- Code required before changing payout or withdrawal details
- Alerts to the owner when a sale is refunded or voided

### Reports and expenses

- Home dashboard with today's sales and key numbers
- Sales, stock and profit reports for any date range, with export
- Expense tracking (rent, transport, utilities)

### Online orders

- Customer shopping app and a shop link (shopynn.app/s/your-shop)
- Customers browse, order and pay with MoMo
- Accept, prepare and complete orders; customers see status updates
- Online order money is tracked and paid out to your MoMo or bank account

### Getting set up

- Go-live checklist: from an empty shop to your first sale in about 15 minutes
- Company profile and logo on receipts and invoices
- Optional assisted setup, where our team helps load products and train staff
- Notifications for sales, low stock and new orders
