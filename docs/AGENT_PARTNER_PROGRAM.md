# Shopynn - Independent Sales Agent Program (Ghana)

**Document purpose:** Terms and expectations for independent field agents who introduce retail and warehouse businesses to Shopynn.

**Version:** 1.0  
**Market:** Ghana  
**Customer payments:** Digital payments only (to Shopynn)

---

## 1. Overview

Shopynn is a subscription-based inventory and business management platform for shops, depots, and multi-branch retailers.

You act as an **independent sales agent**. You introduce businesses you meet in the field, help them understand the product, and support go-live. **All customer fees are paid through Digital channels to Shopynn.** You are paid **commission** separately by Shopynn.

---

## 2. What you sell (summary)

| Plan | Monthly subscription (GHS) | Typical use |
|------|----------------------------|-------------|
| Basic | 229 | Single store, getting started |
| **Standard** | **429** | **Multi-branch, transfers, roles (expected most common)** |
| Premium | 799 | Full platform + customer online ordering |

**One-time onboarding / setup / training:** GHS 2,500 – 6,000 (depending on size and data migration).

Hosting is included in the monthly subscription. Support is via WhatsApp (business hours; priority on Premium).

---

## 3. Your role

You will:

- Visit and introduce Shopynn to qualified businesses (retail, wholesale, depots, etc.).
- Explain plans honestly; do not promise features that are not included on the chosen plan.
- Ensure the business completes payment via **Digital channels** (onboarding and subscription).
- Support initial onboarding handoff to Shopynn for setup and training.
- Use your **agent code** on every registration you originate.

You must **not**:

- Collect subscription or onboarding money on your personal Mobile Money or bank account.
- Change prices or offer discounts without written approval from Shopynn.
- Register duplicate or fake businesses to earn commission.

---

## 4. Commission structure

Commission is calculated on **amounts actually received by Shopynn via Digital channels** (successful payment).

| Fee type | Commission rate | When it applies |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Onboarding / setup fee** | **15%** (one-time) | Once per new business, on the onboarding amount paid |
| **First month subscription** | **10%** (one-time) | On the first subscription payment only |
| **Month 2 and beyond** | **0%** | No commission on subscription renewals |

### Example — Standard plan (most common)

- Onboarding: GHS 4,000 → commission **GHS 600** (15%)
- First month subscription: GHS 429 → commission **GHS 43** (10%)
- **Total commission (if both paid): approximately GHS 643**

The same percentages apply to other plans, based on the amounts actually paid.

---

## 5. When commission is earned and paid

**Commission is earned when:**

- Digital channels show **successful payment** for onboarding and/or subscription; and
- The business is attributed to your **agent code**; and
- Shopynn may require the account to remain **active for 30 days** before final release of onboarding commission (see clawback).

**Payment to you:**

- Settlement **monthly** (or within **14 days after month-end**, as confirmed by Shopynn).
- Paid by **bank transfer or Mobile Money** to your nominated account.
- You receive a **commission statement** listing each business, fees paid, percentages, and amount due.

**Optional payment split (if Shopynn applies):**

- 50% of commission after Digital channel success;
- 50% after the tenant is still **active for 30 days**.

---

## 6. Clawback (adjustments)

Commission may be **reduced or reversed** if:

- Payment is **refunded** or charged back;
- The business **cancels within 30 days** of first payment without genuine use;
- Fraud or misrepresentation is identified.

---

## 7. Lead attribution and territories

- Each agent receives a unique code (e.g. **AGT-001**).
- The agent who **first registers** the prospect with Shopynn owns the commission for that business.
- Territories or focus areas may be agreed (e.g. by city or trade type) to reduce overlap.
- Disputes are resolved by Shopynn using registration date and internal records.

---

## 8. Customer payment — Digital channels only

| Party | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Business (customer)** | Pays onboarding and subscription through **Digital channels** to Shopynn |
| **Agent** | Does not keep customer subscription or onboarding money |
| **Shopynn** | Pays your commission from revenue received |

You may help the shop complete a digital channel payment on-site (e.g. on their phone), but payment must complete to **Shopynn’s Payment channels**, not your personal wallet.

---

## 9. Suggested targets (penetration phase)

A reasonable working target per agent:

- **3–5 new paying businesses per month** (Standard-heavy), with real go-live and handoff to Shopynn support.

Quality and retention matter more than unsigned trials.

---

- WhatsApp support for technical and billing escalations.
- Monthly commission statement and payment.
